# For you lists every card waiting on you

The page header now has **Board · For you N**. For you lists every card from Refine through Ship that needs you,
across all projects, oldest first, with one line saying what it wants and a link to the card. A waiting question or
permission is answered right on the list. Spec: `docs/specs/2026-11-27-for-you.md`.

## Detail
- **Listed:** `state` is `needs-you`, the lane is neither Ideas nor Done, and the lane is built. Ideas are left out
  even though their board tiles show ● needs you, so the count can be lower than the number of badges.
- **What it wants** is worked out by `board/forYou.ts` from what the card stores, first match winning: an open
  question or permission, then an error line last in the lane's chat (as "stuck", in the hive's own words), then the
  lane's default. Spec's default depends on the review: fresh, stale or neither.
- **An error line from an earlier visit to a lane doesn't count.** That holds only while every way out of a lane that
  can leave an error line last writes a line in that lane first. Ship's Back to Try it now writes "Back to Try it.",
  as Back to Build already wrote its own line. A new way out of a lane has to keep this.
- **`cards.waiting_since`** is set in `HiveDb.updateCard` when a card becomes `needs-you` or changes lane while it is.
  Cards already in the database got their newest history row's time, or `created_at`.
- **Answering stays with the card page** for everything but questions and permissions. Land on main, Retry, Back to
  Build, Move on and Sign off depend on what the card page shows next to them. The list reuses `PendingCard` as it is
  (pick, then Send), not the mockup's one click per option.
- **Live:** one `ForYouProvider` feeds the header count and the page. It reloads `GET /api/for-you` on reconnect and
  on `card.updated`, `card.deleted`, `pending.updated`, `spec.updated` and a `chat.message` that is an error line, since
  an error can land on a card that already needs you with no `card.updated`. There's no For you event of its own.
- **Not built:** "Stops nothing", ■ stopped, severe issues, the manager, ◆ versions, and Ship's Approve item. The docs
  are right that pushing at Ship is an Approve item, and it's owed in `docs/CLAUDE_TODO.md`.
- **The For you mockup is out of date in two places:** "Redo the other way" (removed in `2026-11-26-redo-removed`) and
  "rebased" for Ship, which squashes.
