# For you: every card waiting on you, oldest first

**For you** is a page that lists every card waiting for you, across all projects, oldest first. Each entry says in one
line what the card wants and opens it. A waiting question or permission is answered right on the list. The page
header gets a **For you** link with a live count, next to **Board**. Nothing new decides whether a card waits: the
list is the cards the hive already marks `needs-you`, and what each one wants is worked out from what the card
already stores. The one thing added is when the card started waiting.

## Outcome

**What you get:**

- **A For you page** listing every card from Refine through Ship that waits for you, oldest first, with its lane,
  project, title, how long it has waited and one line of what it wants.
- **Questions answered on the list:** a session's question or permission shows its options in place, and your answer
  goes to the session that asked, as it does from the card page.
- **Board · For you N** in the header of every page. The count is live.

**How to verify:**

- **The list:** with one card in Try it and another in Refine waiting for your reply, open For you. Both are listed,
  the one that started waiting first on top. Each shows its lane, project, title, how long ago it started waiting and
  what it wants. The header reads "For you 2".
- **Answer in place:** when a planner, developer or product owner asks a question, it appears on For you with its
  options. Pick one and press Send. The entry leaves the list, the count drops by one, and the card's chat shows your
  answer while the session carries on.
- **It stays live:** reply to a Refine card from its card page. The count drops while the product owner works and goes
  back up when it replies, without a reload.
- **What's left out:** ideas and landed cards never appear. A card that Check stopped after round 2 appears as
  "Check · stuck", with the hive's own line about it.

## Key decisions

- **One entry per waiting card** (reuses). A card is listed when its `state` is `needs-you`, its lane is Refine
  through Ship, and the lane is implemented. That's every place the board shows ● needs you, except Ideas. No new
  card state: in this code `needs-you` already means nothing is running, so the card can't go on without you.
- **What it wants is worked out, not stored** (new). A pure function in `packages/backend/src/board/forYou.ts` takes
  the card's lane, its open pending item, its lane's last chat line and the spec's review state, and returns a kind
  and one line (Design: What each entry says). It doesn't parse prose: an error line's text is the hive's own sentence,
  shown as it is.
- **`Card.waitingSince`** (new). Set when a card starts waiting for you, or changes lane while it waits. It's set in
  `HiveDb.updateCard` and `insertCard`, which every state change goes through, including restart recovery, so no call
  site can forget it. `ARCHITECTURE.md`'s Data Model gains the field.
- **Questions and permissions answered in place; everything else opens the card** (reuses). The list renders the card
  page's `PendingCard` as it is, pick then Send, and posts to the existing `POST /api/for-you/:itemId`. It isn't the
  mockup's one click per option: the form handles several questions, multi-select and your own words, and a question
  looks the same in both places. Land on main, Retry, Back to Build,
  Move on and Sign off stay on the card page, because each one depends on what's shown next to it: uncommitted work and
  the test command at Ship, the review's freshness at Spec, the error line in Build and Check.
- **One list for the header and the page, refetched on events** (new). A `ForYouProvider` at the app root loads
  `GET /api/for-you` and loads it again when the stream reconnects and on `card.updated`, `card.deleted`,
  `pending.updated`, `spec.updated` and a `chat.message` that is an error line; the newest response wins. No new SSE
  event: what an entry says depends on the pending item, the chat and the spec on disk, and no single event carries
  all of those.
- **`GET /api/for-you` returns `{ items }`** (extends `ARCHITECTURE.md`, API). The route the docs planned, as an object,
  so "Stops nothing" can join later as a sibling of `items`.
- **Header navigation** (extends). `Header` gains two `NavLink`s, **Board** and **For you**. The count sits in a pill
  in `--color-accent`, the token kept for it, and is hidden at zero.

## Goals

- One place that shows everything holding up a card, so nothing waits unseen in a project you aren't looking at.
- The quickest kind of wait, a question, answered without leaving the list.

## Non-goals

- **The "Stops nothing" column:** decided for you, suggested rules, found along the way, and what the manager
  answered. Its parts aren't built, apart from decisions, which Try it lists already.
- **Items for parts that don't exist yet:** severe issues, loops, the manager's recommendation and ◆ Mockup versions.
- **■ stopped.** `2026-11-27-check-lane-reviewer` put it "with the For you view and the manager". It stays with the
  manager and severe issues, since it needs ways out of the stopped state that aren't designed yet (`docs/CLAUDE_TODO.md`).
  Until then a stopped Check is a "stuck" entry.
- **Ship's Approve item.** The docs are right that the push at Ship is an Approve item (`PendingItem` kind `approve`).
  The code's Land on main button stands in for it, and building it is Ship's work. Until then a Ship card is listed by
  its lane and opens the card.
- **The mockup's other header items:** Lanes, Rulebooks, Projects, Plan usage and New idea.
- **Ideas, and the board's idea tiles.** Ideas aren't listed, though their tiles still show ● needs you, so the For
  you count can be lower than the number of ● badges on the board.
- **A count in the browser tab, or notifications** (`CONCEPT.md` puts notifications outside the page out of scope).

## Design

The look is `docs/mockups/ForYou.dc.html`, its left column only.

### Which cards are listed

`waitsForYou(card)` in `board/forYou.ts`: `state === "needs-you"`, the lane is neither `ideas` nor `done`, and
`laneImplemented(lane)`. Ideas wait for you by design and hold nothing up; a landed card is marked `needs-you` after
its landing but needs nothing. A card has one entry, however many reasons it has to wait: the first match below wins.

### What each entry says

First match wins:

| What the card holds | Tag | One line | Button |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| An open question | `<Lane> · question` | The first question's text, with "and 1 more" when there are several | The question form, and Open the card |
| An open permission | `<Lane> · permission` | "Allow `<tool>`?" | Allow and Deny, and Open the card |
| An error line last in the lane's chat | `<Lane> · stuck` | The error line's message | Open the card |
| Refine | `Refine · your turn` | "Reply to the product owner, or say Good." | Open the chat |
| Spec, review `fresh` | `Spec · sign off` | "Reviewed. Sign off, or reply to the spec writer." | Open the chat |
| Spec, review `stale` | `Spec · review again` | "The spec changed since its review. Press Review again, or reply." | Open the chat |
| Spec, otherwise | `Spec · your turn` | "Reply to the spec writer, or press Review when the spec is ready." | Open the chat |
| Try it | `Try it` | "Ready to test." | Try it |
| Ship | `Ship · land on main` | "Ready to land on main." | Open the card |
| Anything else | `<Lane>` (kind `waiting`) | "Nothing is running. Open the card to carry on." | Open the card |

The last row shouldn't happen: a Plan, Build or Check card that waits always has a question or an error line. It's
there so an entry never comes out blank.

**An error line from an earlier visit to the lane doesn't count.** A lane's chat keeps every visit, so the rule holds
only if every way out of a lane that can leave an error line last writes a line in that lane first, as Ship's Back to
Build already does ("Back to Build: …"). Back to Try it is the one that doesn't. It gains a Ship note,
"Back to Try it.", so a card whose landing failed and that you accept again reads "Ship · land on main", not the old
failure. The card page's Landing chat then shows the same thing.

Every button except the question form is a link to `/cards/:id`, so it can open in a new tab. The spec writer asks you
the review's decisions as questions, so "Spec · question" covers the mockup's review decisions.

### The API

`GET /api/for-you` returns `ForYouResponse { items: ForYouItem[] }`, sorted by `since`, then by when the card was
created. These go in `packages/shared`:

```ts
type ForYouKind =
  | "question" | "permission" | "stuck" | "your-turn" | "sign-off" | "review-again" | "try-it" | "land" | "waiting";

interface ForYouItem {
  card: CardSummary;
  projectName: string;
  /** When the card started waiting for you (Card.waitingSince). */
  since: string;
  kind: ForYouKind;
  /** What the card wants, in one line. */
  text: string;
  /** The open question or permission, answered on the list. Null for every other kind. */
  pending: PendingItem | null;
}
```

`Hive.forYou()` collects each listed card's inputs: `db.openPendingFor`, `db.lastChatItem` for its lane, and, in Spec,
the review state from `refreshSpec`, which reads the file from disk as the card page does. The derivation is the pure
function, so its table above is tested without a hive.

### When a card started waiting

A new `cards.waiting_since` column, added the way `ADDED_COLUMNS` adds the others. When `updateCard` writes `state` or
`lane` and the card ends up `needs-you`, it sets `waiting_since` to now, unless the card was already `needs-you` in the
same lane. `insertCard` sets it for a new card. So:

- in Refine and Spec it's the end of the session's last turn: your turn since then;
- a question that arrives mid-turn restarts it, since the card goes from working to needs-you;
- accepting in Try it restarts it in Ship, as a new thing is wanted;
- a hive restart that marks a cut-off card `needs-you` restarts it from that moment.

Cards already waiting when the column is added take the time of their newest `events` row, or `created_at` if they
have none.

### The page

A new route `/for-you` and `pages/ForYou.tsx`, under the shared `Header`. It shows:

- **The heading:** "For you", and below it "3 things are holding up a card, oldest first. Everything else is running on
  its own." With nothing listed: "Nothing is waiting for you. Everything is running on its own."
- **One entry per item,** in a column at a reading width (a new layout token). As in the mockup, each entry is a white
  block with a border: the tag in the ● needs-you colors, then `project · title` in bold, how long ago on the right
  ("2 h ago", redrawn every minute), the one line, and its buttons.
- **A question or permission entry** renders `PendingCard` under the line. A failed answer shows its error in the entry,
  and an answer someone already gave elsewhere shows the server's "That has already been answered." Entries are keyed
  by card id, so a refetch caused by another card never clears an answer you've half typed.
- **A load error** shows the way the board shows one.

### The header

`Header` keeps the name and version, and gains `<nav aria-label="Main">` with **Board** (`/`) and **For you**
(`/for-you`), as `NavLink`s, so the current page gets `aria-current="page"` and the mockup's active look. The For you
link shows the count from `ForYouProvider` in an accent pill when it's above zero, and its accessible name says
"For you, 3 waiting". The Board and card pages already render `Header`, so they get both links with no change.

### Docs this changes

- `ARCHITECTURE.md`, Data Model, Card: `waitingSince (when the card started waiting for you; For you sorts on it)`.
- `docs/CLAUDE_TODO.md`: an owed item for Ship's Approve item, since the code's Land on main button stands in for it.

## Alternatives considered

- **Ideas listed below the cards under way,** so the count matches the board's ● badges. Rejected: an idea holds
  nothing up, and a backlog of them would bury what does.
- **One click per option,** as in the mockup. Rejected: a single-select answer would go at once, with no way to take
  it back, and the list's form would differ from the card page's.

- **Links only** (no answering on the list). Rejected: a one-line question from a developer would cost a trip to the
  card page and back, and the answer route is already the For you route.
- **Everything done from the list,** as the mockup's buttons suggest. Rejected: Land on main, Retry and Sign off each
  depend on what the card page shows next to them, which the list would have to copy, or skip.
- **Membership by pending items only,** as `ARCHITECTURE.md` frames For you. Rejected: Try it, Ship, your turn in
  Refine and Spec, and stuck cards have no pending item, and they're most of what waits.
- **A `foryou.updated` SSE event.** Rejected: every place that changes a card, a chat line or the spec on disk would
  have to send it. A refetch after the events that already exist covers them.
- **Waiting time from the newest history row,** with no new column. Rejected: which rows mark the start of a wait would
  be a rule that every new kind of history row has to keep, and one written while the card already waits would make it
  look newer. The column is set where the state changes, so it's exact. The backfill uses that rule only once, for
  cards already waiting.

## Implementation strategy

*Not part of the design — a starting point for whoever builds this.*

- **Multi-agent: the lead plus 1 chunk.** The lead settles the shared types first (`ForYouItem`, `ForYouKind`,
  `ForYouResponse`), then builds the backend: `waiting_since` in `db/index.ts`, `board/forYou.ts`, `Hive.forYou()`
  and the route. In parallel, a subagent builds the frontend against those types: `ForYouProvider`, the client call,
  `Header`, `pages/ForYou.tsx` and its entry component, and the route in `main.tsx`.
- **Opus 5.5 lead, Sonnet 5.5 for the frontend.** Restarting `waitingSince` at the right moments touches every state
  write and restart recovery. The page and header follow a settled mockup and contract.
