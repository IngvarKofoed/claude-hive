Brief

2 changes in round 3 Select any text to comment on it

Problem

Players can't tell whether a seed's price is high or low right now, so they sell at random and feel cheated when the price jumps the next day.

Who it's for

Players deciding when to sell their seeds. changed: was "players and the game designer"

What done looks like

  • Each seed in the shop shows its price history, one point per day. changed
  • Today's price is marked against the lowest and highest price in the period shown.
  • Open: how far back the history goes. Suggested: 30 days.

Out of scope

  • Price predictions or advice on when to sell
  • History for anything other than seeds
Rules applied from the magic-grow rulebook “Prices are shown in coins, never as percentages.”
