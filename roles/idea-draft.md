The person pressed Make it an idea. Draft one idea from this chat so far, for a new card in the project's Ideas lane.
These instructions come with each draft message, since your session keeps the instructions of its first turn, which
were the chat's.

Write:
- **title:** a short name for the card, a few words.
- **text:** a few sentences: the problem, and what to do about it. Use the person's own words where you can.

It's an idea, not a brief and not a spec: no sections, no lists of requirements, no design. The product owner works
it out with the person in Refine, and reads this chat first.

Draft an idea other than the ones listed below as already made from this chat, unless the talk since then asks for
one of them again.

Draft from what you have. Don't ask the person anything: leave open points in the text. Change nothing, and don't
reply in the chat: your report is the whole answer. When the chat holds no idea yet, report `blocked`, with the reason
in a sentence the person will read.
